### Rubric

- **S — specific:** the visible text fits its topic, card and spread position; it
  names an observable situation rather than only an abstract keyword.
- **U — unexpected:** there is an image, turn or useful call-out beyond familiar
  advice. Ominous imagery is welcome; unsupported claims are not.
- **Q — quotable:** at least one natural Thai line can stand alone without needing
  the reader to decode a template join.
- **W — worth a screenshot:** I would share a visible passage without first having
  to explain away an irrelevant line or a contradictory slot.
- A reading passes with at least **three of four**, including **S**. A material
  topic/position contradiction fails it even when individual jokes are good.
  Gentle can pass through a quiet, memorable observation; it need not roast.

## First-pass observations retained for traceability

- Raw keywords such as “ฟื้นจากเศร้า”, “เริ่มเปิดรับ” and “ชวนคุยด้วยใจ” are useful
  data labels but often unnatural spoken headlines. Quotation marks solve joining,
  not topic relevance. Prefer a card/topic observation for the headline when one is
  available, with the keyword as a secondary tag.
- The three weekly exam tips repeat across many samples. Keep the exam-window
  safety logic, but connect at least one tip to this draw's actual preparation
  problem, and avoid printing the same persona prefix twice in one reading.
- Spice-3 verdicts repeat the selected hard truth verbatim. A cliffhanger is more
  convincing when it opens a consequence or unanswered choice attached to this
  draw, rather than telling every positive Sun reader they have a hidden matter
  waiting to be discussed.
- The sample generator should omit per-card bites below spice 3, or clearly label
  them as hidden audit fields. Otherwise the editorial review is easier than the
  actual visitor experience.
- No new direct predictions of illness, death, pregnancy, money outcomes, actual
  cheating or exam results were found in these samples. Do not sacrifice that
  restraint while fixing the fun score.
